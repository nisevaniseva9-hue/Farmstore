🎁 Want to test REAL WhatsApp messages sent directly from Meta to your phone for free?

Meta provides a 100% free developer sandbox with test credits. Follow these 3 steps to connect it:

Step 1: Open Meta Dashboard

Go to developers.facebook.com > My Apps > select your App > WhatsApp API Setup.

Step 2: Add Your Personal Phone

In the "To" dropdown on Meta, click Manage phone number list and add your personal WhatsApp number (Meta sends a 6-digit OTP to verify).

Step 3: Paste Test Credentials

In .env, set WHATSAPP_PROVIDER=meta, paste the Temporary Access Token and Phone number ID from Meta, and restart the server!
